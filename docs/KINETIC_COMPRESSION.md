# Kinetic Compression

## Status

Active Story Brainstorm research lens, commissioned 2026-09-05.

## Goal

Find narrative mechanisms where one concrete action, motion, object, omission, or consequence performs several story jobs at once. Prefer causal overlap over sequential explanation.

Weak shape: `characterization -> exposition -> threat -> conflict -> consequence`

Compressed shape: `one consequential act changes several state variables at once`

## Already exposed in the corpus

- **Causal Shadow** — effects arrive before acts; environment and other people reveal a character's hidden reach.
- **Counter-Mask** — reassurance and threat occupy the same behavior; charm becomes evidence of active perception management.
- **Distributed Command** — power is carried in other people's behavior, keeping an absent or quiet character causally present.
- **Domain Capture** — ordinary space or procedure is discovered to have been captured before the protagonist knew there was a contest.

These are not four disconnected tricks. They expose a common compression law: **causal overlap**. A good beat does not merely symbolize several things; it changes several meaningful story states at once.

## Drew stipulation: economy of motion

The reference image is Jackie Chan closing a door gently and smoothly to engineer stealth: competence becomes visible through removal of wasted acceleration, collision, correction, and noise.

Research questions:
- How much story can one movement carry?
- Can stillness do more work than exertion?
- Can a tiny motion transmit command, competence, danger, restraint, relationship, and tactical state at once?
- Can preparatory precision delete later motion?
- Does the character prove control by refusing unnecessary action?

## The shovel

The shovel is the standing object test for kinetic compression. Ask what it physically does, what prior action has marked it, what relationship or ownership it carries, what future action its presence makes possible, whether its role can change without replacing the object, and whether it preserves consequences after the actor leaves.

Sometimes a shovel is just a shovel. This lane is interested in cases where it is simultaneously tool, scar, leverage, memory, threat, infrastructure, and evidence.

## Current direction

Re-mine already-opened works before expanding the bibliography. The first pass proved danger-through-causal-shadow; subsequent passes should look for missed compression in motion, stillness, props, scars, role changes, and actions that carry several state transitions simultaneously.

Do not impose a fixed source count. Do not canonize from a single work when cross-source comparison is available. Preserve exact short evidence and separate observation from inference.
