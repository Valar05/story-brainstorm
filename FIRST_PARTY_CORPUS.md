# First-Party Author Corpus

This directory is deliberately separate from the public-domain primitive corpus.

It contains analysis of Drew Clarke's own works, beginning with **Vanguard of Glory**.

## Why this lane exists

The public-domain lane extracts small, transferable mechanisms with short evidence and strict derivative-risk constraints.

The first-party lane has a different job:

- preserve detailed scene mechanics;
- preserve exact combat causality;
- preserve character voice and social texture;
- preserve economic, equipment, travel, rank, injury, and recovery logic;
- preserve failed attempts and corrections;
- extract reusable story/combat grammar without flattening the source into vague "influence";
- provide source-grounded material for downstream campaign bibles and game writing.

Because this is first-party material, analysis may be substantially more detailed than the public-domain cards. However, **source event, mechanism, and downstream canon remain separate states**. A later project does not silently inherit Vanguard of Glory biography or events.

## Hard separation

Public-domain accepted corpus:
`data/primitive_cards.json`

First-party author corpus:
`first_party/`

Do not merge first-party records into the 1,000-card PD quota.

## First work

Vanguard of Glory is a foundation source for Vanguard's combat and practical-world grammar. Its combat should be analyzed at scene, exchange, force-path, decision, and consequence level rather than compressed into one vague card.
