# Vanguard of Glory — First-Party Deep Extraction Doctrine

## Purpose

Vanguard of Glory is not a vague influence file.

It is a **first-party foundation corpus** for Vanguard's story, combat, practical-world logic, and voice.

The public-domain primitive system intentionally compresses source evidence into small reusable cards. That level of compression is insufficient here.

For Vanguard of Glory, preserve the machinery at the level at which it was written.

## Extraction resolution

Every important scene may be decomposed across all of these axes:

### 1. Story causality
- entry state
- local objective
- pressure
- decision
- reversal
- consequence
- residue carried into later scenes

### 2. Character state
- what Vanguard knows
- what she misreads
- what she fears
- what she wants materially
- what she refuses
- what she learns
- what costs her pride, comfort, safety, money, equipment, advancement, or time

### 3. Combat exchange grammar

Do not summarize a fight as "Vanguard wins with clever use of Aether."

Record:
- initial range;
- stance;
- weapon orientation;
- hand occupancy;
- footing;
- line of attack;
- force source;
- force destination;
- what is committed;
- what remains free;
- which body or equipment layer receives load;
- whether she redirects, absorbs, evades, binds, grapples, or overpowers;
- what Aether/essence is activated;
- ramp time;
- resource cost;
- hit result;
- position after contact;
- mistake;
- adaptation;
- recovery state.

Each exchange should be reconstructable as physical choreography.

### 4. Kinetic compression

Record whenever an exchange collapses several variables into one decisive state transition:
- footing creates opening;
- mass prevents redirection;
- sharpness concentrates force;
- armor buys one mistake;
- enemy commitment creates flank;
- grip/weapon ownership changes;
- environmental geometry changes what attacks are possible.

### 5. Practical-world texture

Preserve:
- food;
- baths;
- laundry;
- armor cleaning;
- weapon maintenance;
- stables;
- pack animals;
- roads;
- room prices;
- guild fees;
- market monopolies;
- bargaining;
- rank recognition;
- paperwork;
- local gossip;
- travel supplies;
- essence storage;
- potion preparation;
- exhaustion and rest.

These are not decorative details. They establish the material reality Vanguard fights inside.

### 6. Social mechanics

Record:
- who has formal authority;
- who has practical authority;
- how strangers become legible;
- rank/status signals;
- registration;
- hospitality;
- intimidation;
- bargaining;
- rumor;
- local monopoly;
- institutional coercion;
- how violence is socially contained or normalized.

### 7. Voice

Preserve:
- Vanguard's directness;
- dry humor;
- practical noticing;
- impatience with institutional friction;
- willingness to threaten without grandstanding;
- appetite;
- bodily discomfort;
- money awareness;
- gear awareness;
- tactical self-criticism.

### 8. Combat mistakes are first-class evidence

A good Vanguard scene often includes a real mistake followed by adaptation.

Never sanitize these away.

Examples from recovered source:
- overcommitting Mass makes redirection difficult;
- failing to reinforce her own body lets enemy momentum bear her down;
- superior armor does not replace footing;
- another fighter can look smoother and more economical, giving Vanguard a visible standard to grow toward.

### 9. Downstream use

Downstream projects may inherit:
- questions;
- mechanisms;
- combat grammar;
- material logic;
- voice tendencies;
- structural pressures.

They do not inherit source events, names, geography, power-system nouns, or biography unless explicitly promoted.

## Desired density

A major fight may deserve dozens of exchange records.

A social chapter may deserve separate records for:
- arrival;
- gate inspection;
- registration;
- inn;
- bath/repair;
- trade;
- rumor;
- confrontation;
- aftermath.

This is intentionally much denser than the 10-card public-domain extraction model.
