## Combat-cultivation architecture

The earlier combat-law extraction is necessary but incomplete. Vanguard of Glory treats combat as one part of a larger material loop that includes training, cultivation, economy, ecology, equipment, recovery, and social consequence.

### Whole combat stack

A Vanguard encounter can operate across these layers simultaneously:

1. **World / motive**
   - why the fight is happening
   - ecology, law, faction, reputation, job, bounty, invasive species, road safety, local politics
   - what victory or failure changes outside the arena

2. **Progression / cultivation**
   - rank
   - Cladding
   - Essence Binding / Tempering
   - permanent vs consumable essence
   - residual Aether built through training
   - physical conditioning without infusion
   - learned technique and conceptual understanding

3. **Resource economy**
   - personal Aether reserves
   - ambient Aether density and draw rate
   - equipment attunement
   - Aether batteries
   - potions
   - consumable essence
   - fatigue, hunger, water, preparation time

4. **Perception / information**
   - normal senses
   - enhanced hearing, smell, sight
   - aura sight
   - rank and infusion reading
   - hidden threats
   - opponent habits and equipment dependencies
   - tracking and terrain readability

5. **Material / equipment**
   - armor composition
   - weapon composition
   - conductivity
   - capacity
   - ramp-up time
   - structural damage
   - repair state
   - pack capacity and what can actually be carried

6. **Technique / body**
   - stance
   - guard
   - grip
   - footwork
   - weapon class conversion
   - body infusion
   - air / terrain manipulation
   - shield work
   - grappling
   - learned named techniques

7. **Immediate physical state**
   - range
   - footing
   - facing
   - balance
   - contact
   - weapon ownership
   - armor integrity
   - wound state
   - wall / terrain interaction
   - recoil and recovery debt

8. **Aftermath**
   - harvest
   - loot
   - repair
   - food and sleep
   - reputation
   - advancement gained or lost
   - emotional residue
   - what the next scene now costs

The melee ontology belongs inside this stack. It does not replace it.

### Combat reasoning loop

Vanguard's characteristic combat reasoning can be modeled as:

**OBSERVE -> INFER -> TEST -> EXPLOIT -> PAY COST -> UPDATE MODEL**

Examples from first-party material:
- aura sight reveals hidden archers and rank
- uninfused boots reveal an Aether-manipulation opening
- a battery explains impossible throughput
- a telegraphed swing reveals timing
- a cracked plate becomes a later penetration route
- an opponent's poor defense becomes visible after their throughput advantage is removed

This is adaptive reasoning, not combo execution.

### Advancement can be spent in combat

A critical first-party rule: combat can consume the fighter's future.

Before Essence Binding, Mass or Sharpness essence may be advancement material. Spending it for survival can delay Bronze progression by weeks or months.

Therefore resource cost can be measured in:
- current Aether
- potion stock
- equipment attunement
- repair burden
- money
- essence crystals
- future rank progress
- physical fatigue
- social debt

The strongest combat choice is not always the cheapest future choice.

### Progression is experiential

The cultivation sequence is:

**experience -> concept -> meditation -> partial binding -> experimentation -> tactical incorporation -> permanent identity**

Mass and Sharpness are not merely unlocked statistics.

Vanguard meditates on concrete experiences:
- body weight behind a shield bash
- blades cutting through Malformed
- impact, edge, commitment, penetration

Then she tests the bound concepts:
- Sharpness in air can fail structurally
- Sharpness on the hand can create a limited edge without making flesh itself sharp
- the shield rim accepts Sharpness readily
- Mass makes initiation harder but amplifies momentum once moving
- Mass applied late to a moving limb or object can create violent impact
- Mass + Sharpness produces Wedge behavior: force concentrated through an interface

New concepts should begin as hypotheses with discoverable affordances.

### Concepts obey physical laws

**Mass**
- increases inertia / commitment
- makes movement harder to start
- amplifies momentum and impact once moving
- can improve anchoring
- creates redirection and recovery debt

**Sharpness**
- concentrates force at an edge or interface
- can alter Aether structures and compatible objects
- does not simply make living flesh itself into a blade
- exploits existing openings exceptionally well

**Wedge**
- not a flat bonus
- a compositional principle: momentum / pressure plus concentrated interface
- especially effective against already compromised structure

### Equipment is style architecture

Material choice is tactical:
- duraluminum: faster conduction, lower protection / capacity, cheaper, useful for split-second armor reinforcement
- steel: slower ramp-up, greater capacity, appropriate for sword and shield storage / heavy conduction

Attunement is also state:
- a shield can spend stored Aether to extend its guard
- weapon overcharge can project edge behavior
- draining attunement changes later defensive or offensive options

Artifacts can be both upgrades and warnings.
The stolen Aether battery gives throughput and reduces strain, but Grimsteel demonstrates the dependency failure mode. Useful loot can therefore carry a visible temptation.

### Terrain and ecology are combat systems

The Chitter hunt demonstrates that combat begins before contact:
- tracking
- scout removal
- aura vision tradeoffs
- improvised projectile use
- earthwork cover
- funnel creation
- crowd management
- enemy diet / rageroot behavior
- pack structure
- nursing broodmother
- post-battle essence harvest

Environment may provide:
- rocks
- air
- earth
- walls
- corpses
- cover
- choke points
- loose weapons
- enemy equipment

The battlefield is inventory.

### Recovery is part of combat

A fight is not over when the enemy dies.

Recovery includes:
- washing blood and mud away
- cleaning / drying / oiling armor
- checking hidden structural damage
- replacing packing
- preparing potions
- eating because Aether expenditure raises hunger
- drinking because meditation and integration dehydrate
- sleeping
- deciding whether to remain armored
- paying for labor and lodging

This is not downtime garnish. It closes the resource loop.

### Social and economic consequences

Combat competence becomes local infrastructure:
- free meals
- lodging
- provisions
- a mule
- public reputation
- bargaining leverage
- guild hostility
- dueling status
- legal recognition
- predatory attention

Reputation is local, concrete, and service-mediated rather than an abstract renown bar.

### Writing rule

For prose and game design:

**Every meaningful combat beat should change at least one tactical fact and one broader cost or opportunity.**

Examples:
- parry changes line and drains attunement
- Mass strike breaks stance and spends advancement material
- aura sight reveals an artifact but makes terrain harder to read
- potion restores reserves but consumes an hour of prior preparation
- victory yields essence but creates a carrying-capacity problem
- structural injury creates both the next attack route and the later repair bill

### Full Vanguard loop

**LIVE -> ENCOUNTER -> MODEL -> FIGHT -> CONSEQUENCE -> RECOVER -> REFLECT -> BIND -> EXPERIMENT -> INTEGRATE**

Around the full loop sits:

**ECONOMY / SOCIETY / ECOLOGY**

A fight concludes only when we know what Vanguard carried out of it into the person she is becoming.
