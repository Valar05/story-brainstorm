# Vanguard of Glory — Combat Bible

## Prime law

Combat is a chain of state changes.

A strong exchange answers:
1. What state exists before the move?
2. What action changes it?
3. What new problem does that create?
4. What does the fighter notice?
5. What becomes newly possible?

The writing rarely treats attacks as isolated spectacle.

## Public duel in Layowich

### Initial state
- large fighter: superior raw strength, full-body Aether, relentless pressure
- smaller fighter: conserved energy, lateral movement, deflection, patience

### Exchange
1. Larger fighter attacks repeatedly.
2. Smaller fighter spends less, sidestepping and deflecting.
3. Frustration produces a huge overhead swing.
4. Smaller fighter alters the ground under the attacker's intended foot placement.
5. Attacker's base fails as commitment peaks.
6. Defender steps outside the line.
7. Defender sweeps the unbalanced leg.
8. Free hand shoves.
9. Attacker drops saber to catch himself.
10. Fight changes category from weapon duel to grapple.
11. Smaller fighter wins the scramble.
12. Yield ends the contest.
13. The handshake contains one last dominance test.
14. Smaller fighter quietly answers the crush with Aether-enhanced grip.

### Combat law
Victory begins before the final attack.

The decisive move is not "strong sweep."
It is:
patience -> commitment bait -> footing edit -> angle -> sweep -> grappling conversion.

## Courtyard duel: source rules

The important structure is that Vanguard enters prepared:
- sword and shield infused
- armor infused to deny hostile Aether holds
- she waits to read the opponent
- armor choice is justified by prior bandit experience, not fashion

This encodes:
**preparation is combat.**

## Alpha wolf exchange

This is the strongest source example of kinetic causal combat.

### State 0
- alpha: larger than normal wolf, six-legged / extra-limbed, bone plated
- Blaze's spear already proves the armor can deflect a normal thrust
- pack is entering
- Vanguard takes alpha while Blaze isolates pack

### Beat 1: reverse grip / murder-strike
Vanguard reverses the sword and attacks with hilt/pommel geometry rather than edge.

Function:
- target is armored
- blunt transfer is more useful than trying to cut plating

### Beat 2: acceleration
She pulls from Aether Battery.
Each sprinting step compounds forward commitment.

Function:
- velocity is built over steps
- the attack is prepared bodily, not summoned abstractly

### Beat 3: jaw catch
Alpha catches the weapon in its jaws.

This is not just "block."
It creates a mechanical bind around the weapon.

### Beat 4: Mass overcharge
Vanguard makes the weapon too massive for the alpha to support.

Outcome:
- wolf + weapon hit ground
- teeth lost
- lower jaw plate cracks

Critical law:
**the first successful hit creates a persistent structural defect.**

### Beat 5: overcommitment
Vanguard follows with another overhead smash.
Alpha dodges.

Mass now becomes debt:
- heavy swing resists redirection
- she has to pull essence back
- weapon is out of position during recovery

Critical law:
**power changes handling.**

Power is not a number added to damage.
It alters what the fighter can do next.

### Beat 6: punishment
Alpha lunges while weapon is displaced.
Vanguard infuses armor in time to survive the blow.

But:
- she is physically knocked down
- weapon leaves her hand

Critical law:
**armor prevents catastrophe, not consequence.**

### Beat 7: retrospective error recognition
She realizes she should have infused her body with Mass to remain planted.

This is excellent because the prose teaches the system through error.

### Beat 8: state exploitation
On the ground and disarmed, she does not reset to neutral.
She punches the already-cracked jaw/skull plate.

The uppercut exploits damage created several beats earlier.

Critical law:
**damage should remain available to later decisions.**

### Beat 9: weapon-class conversion
She draws dagger.
Sharpness goes into the edge.
Aether creates a projected lance.

The attack targets the newly opened armor gap.

### Beat 10: kill
Dagger enters through the damaged gap and penetrates brain.

The finisher exists because:
- first impact cracked armor
- uppercut enlarged failure
- dagger is smaller and better suited to the available opening
- Sharpness concentrates force through the gap

Critical law:
**finishers emerge from state, not from cooldown.**

### Beat 11: redundant certainty
She retrieves sword and continues crushing the head.

This is ugly, practical confirmation.

The fight is not written as elegant sport.
It is survival.

## Blaze contrast

Blaze's pack-fighting demonstrates mature economy:
- every motion has a purpose
- a low lunge manipulates another enemy's leap
- an impaled body becomes a collision weapon against another wolf
- reach manages multiple bodies
- fire wall separates combat spaces

Vanguard explicitly feels clumsy by comparison.

This provides the origin-story calibration:
Vanguard is already dangerous.
She is not yet the most technically economical fighter in the room.

## Combat laws extracted

1. Preparation is part of combat.
2. Footing is a weapon.
3. Overcommitment is punishable.
4. Force has handling debt.
5. Armor is margin, not strategy.
6. Damage persists structurally.
7. Mistakes create the next problem.
8. Good fighters convert category: weapon -> grapple, bind -> throw, armor crack -> penetration.
9. The best move is often the one that changes what moves are possible next.
10. Resource use should alter future options.
11. Environmental separation can reshape a multi-enemy fight.
12. Bodies can become tools against other bodies.
13. Economy reads as mastery.
14. A fighter can be powerful and still visibly learning.
15. Finishers are consequences of state.

## Gravity Fist / Inner Heart transfer

For the new game:
- kinetic compression should preserve this causal chain
- shoulder charge should change footing / wall / enemy pose state
- bayonet hook should create control state
- pull should create point-blank line
- blast should exploit that line
- recoil should create recovery debt
- armor should let Vanguard survive errors without erasing displacement or knockdown
- visible cracks, staggers, limb position, wall contact, prone state, weapon ownership, and guard state should remain available to later moves
