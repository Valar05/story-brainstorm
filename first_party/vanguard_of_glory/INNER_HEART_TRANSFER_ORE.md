## 9. Transfer the full combat-cultivation loop, not just the melee chain

Inner Heart should inherit the broader Vanguard structure:

**LIVE -> ENCOUNTER -> MODEL -> FIGHT -> CONSEQUENCE -> RECOVER -> REFLECT -> BIND -> EXPERIMENT -> INTEGRATE**

The new halberd / extendable scatter weapon ontology is one tactical vocabulary inside that loop.

Preserve these first-party behaviors in the origin campaign:
- train sometimes without enhancement because unassisted strain matters
- let movement / Aether use build long-term body capability
- make ambient energy density matter
- distinguish personal reserve from stored / attuned equipment energy
- make equipment material affect conduction, capacity, response time, cost, and repair
- let perception modes reveal information while obscuring something else
- let new abilities begin as hypotheses Vanguard tests
- let advancement resources be spendable in emergencies, turning survival into lost future progress
- let food, water, sleep, cleaning, and repair close the combat loop
- let loot create carrying, sale, theft, and reputation problems
- let local society react materially to what Vanguard did

### Inner Heart adaptation

Do not merely rename Aether resources as biomass.

Translate function:
- ambient Heart pressure / nutrient field can alter recovery or ability throughput
- living armor materials can differ in response speed, capacity, compatibility, or appetite
- mutation / symbiote acquisition can have stages analogous to concept integration rather than instant mastery
- new body capabilities should support experimentation scenes
- a capability may have an obvious use, failed use, practical use, and surprising later use
- costly emergency use may consume something intended for long-term growth
- social enclaves should feed, repair, classify, tax, praise, fear, or exploit Vanguard based on witnessed conduct

### Combat reasoning should remain visible

Use:

**OBSERVE -> INFER -> TEST -> EXPLOIT -> PAY COST -> UPDATE MODEL**

Inner Heart opponents should expose readable facts:
- scent dependence
- limb geometry
- pressure interval
- shell weakness
- route habit
- symbiote reliance
- stored charge
- debt mark
- footing preference

Vanguard wins because she notices and changes the problem, not because the prose announces a stronger attack.

### Recovery and progression should cross-pollinate

After a major fight, ask:
- what must be washed or removed?
- what gear or body structure needs repair?
- what did Vanguard consume that was meant for future growth?
- what new tactical concept did the fight teach?
- what can she now test safely?
- what local person or faction now treats her differently?
- what new appetite, dependence, or maintenance burden exists?

The campaign should make new combat capability emerge from lived events, not from detached upgrade menus.
