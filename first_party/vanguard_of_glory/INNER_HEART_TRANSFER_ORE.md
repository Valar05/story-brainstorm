# Vanguard of Glory -> Inner Heart Origin Campaign — Transfer Ore

Status: DESIGN TRANSFER, NOT AUTOMATIC CANON

## 1. Social areas should be transactional worlds

Do not build "safe rooms."

Build places where Vanguard must:
- be classified
- register or refuse
- pay or barter
- eat
- sleep
- clean herself
- repair gear
- hear gossip
- learn monopoly structures
- meet locals through services
- decide whether visible identification is protection or vulnerability

## 2. The Heart can have guild-equivalent institutions without humans

Possible forms:
- pulse-reader caste that identifies clone lineage
- scent-mark registry for travelers
- scar seals showing safe passage
- symbiote accreditation
- caravan organisms controlling nutrient routes
- bonewright monopoly over repairs
- archive tissue that remembers debts
- host collective that grants "safe-to-feed" status

The point is not aesthetic substitution.
The point is local legibility.

## 3. Young Vanguard should learn through other fighters

The Layowich duel and Blaze both provide models she has not fully internalized.

The origin campaign should repeatedly let her see:
- a local fighter whose motion is more economical
- a nonhuman culture solving violence through unfamiliar geometry
- a creature that uses yielding instead of bracing
- someone whose weapon/body relation reveals a better answer

Her legend should grow by stealing good ideas with her eyes.

## 4. Kinetic compression should inherit Wedge logic

Mass:
- commitment
- inertia
- floorward consequence
- reduced redirectability

Sharpness:
- force concentration
- penetration through available opening
- exploitation of structural defect

Kinetic compression:
- gather force into a constrained chain
- preserve contact
- redirect or release at the state change
- accept handling debt

## 5. Combat damage should persist

Examples for game state:
- cracked shell
- bent limb
- opened seam
- pinned weapon
- wall-stuck enemy
- ruptured sac
- staggered foot
- exposed gland
- broken plate
- collapsed support

Later moves should care.

## 6. The Merchant should be an economy before a boss

Harrick works because we transact with him before judging him.

The Merchant should similarly:
- price things
- know supply
- provide something genuinely useful
- exploit scarcity
- remember previous deals
- control access
- possibly be funny
- possibly be hated
- still be worth visiting

The boss fight gains enormous weight if the player has done business with him for hours.

## 7. Social areas should make the dungeon worth saving or at least understanding

If the Inner Heart only contains monsters, destroying it is simple.

If it contains:
- traders
- children-equivalents
- maintenance castes
- escaped clones
- symbiote hosts
- weird cooks
- repairers
- archivists
- pilgrims
- parasites with property disputes

then every campaign decision becomes denser.

## 8. Do not skip the bath

This is shorthand for a larger law:

**recovery should have texture.**

Show:
- what gets washed off
- what still hurts
- what gear needs attention
- what resource was spent
- what has to dry
- what smell will attract predators
- what damage cannot be fixed tonight

That ordinary material layer is part of why Vanguard of Glory feels grounded.
