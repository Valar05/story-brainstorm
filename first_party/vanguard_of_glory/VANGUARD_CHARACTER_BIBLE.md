## Combat-cultivation character loop

Young Vanguard's growth is experiential rather than menu-like.

She repeatedly moves through:

**experience -> concept -> meditation -> experimentation -> incorporation**

Important character consequences:
- she trains without Aether when useful because ordinary muscular wear still matters
- she deliberately alternates infused exertion and uninfused work to build body and residual Aether
- she can spend advancement essence to survive, sacrificing future progress for present life
- she reflects on actual fights when binding new concepts
- she treats new powers as things to test, not things she automatically understands
- she can become tempted by tools that make power easier, while recognizing dependency in other fighters
- she notices hunger, thirst, mental strain, repair burden, and carrying capacity as part of advancement

Her curiosity is practical:
- What does this capability actually do?
- What material accepts it?
- What fails?
- What becomes harder?
- What does it cost to use repeatedly?
- What can I combine it with?

This makes experimentation a character scene, not merely a mechanics tutorial.

The origin campaign should preserve the pleasure of watching Vanguard become more dangerous because she understands more, not because the narrative declares her stronger.
