## Extraction policy

This corpus is not compressed to ten cards per work.

Vanguard of Glory is treated as first-party foundational material and may preserve:
- scene-level causality
- full combat exchange logic
- resource/economy detail
- institutions and social pressure
- voice and joke construction
- equipment state
- environmental state
- mistakes and recovery
- progression logic
- exact mechanism transfer notes

No public-domain rights gate applies to the author's own work. Provenance still matters.

## Canon boundary

Source Vanguard of Glory events are not silently made canonical to later Fleshpunk continuity.
Mechanisms may transfer freely when explicitly cited.
Character identity, biography, institutions, geography, and exact events require explicit promotion.
